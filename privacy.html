Dialed In - Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026. Developer: Arthur Joyce.

Dialed In does not require an account. The app does not collect or send your personal data to us, uses no advertising or analytics tools, and does not track you.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved sounds, notes, setlists, photos and recordings are stored in the app on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • Microphone: used only when you choose to record a short clip of a sound, or to say your settings out loud. Spoken settings are turned into text on your device by Apple's on-device speech recognition. If it isn't available on your device, you can type your settings instead.
  • Photos and camera: used only when you choose to attach a photo of your rig. The app does not browse your photo library.
  • Sharing: if you tap share, Apple's share sheet sends what you choose, to whom you choose. The app doesn't see who you share with.

These permissions are optional; the library and manual entry work without them, and you can turn them off any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Keeping and deleting your content

Your content stays in the app until you delete it or delete the app. Device backups (iCloud or your computer) may keep a copy according to your backup settings; we cannot access those backups.

Email and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and message through our email provider and use them only to reply. We don't sell them or use them for advertising. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps visitors' IP addresses in security logs; we add no analytics or ads. Links to sources open other websites with their own privacy policies.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
